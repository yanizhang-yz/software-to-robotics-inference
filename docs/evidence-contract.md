# Evidence Contract

## Learn

Record the concepts, sources, and assumptions that informed the work.

## Build

Commit the runnable implementation, configuration, and setup instructions.

## Measure

Publish the command, environment, inputs, outputs, and limitations behind a
measurement or evaluation.

## Present

Explain the artifact and its results so another engineer can assess the work.

## Status Vocabulary

- `planned` — scoped but no runnable artifact exists;
- `active` — implementation or measurement is in progress;
- `blocked` — a named dependency prevents progress; and
- `verified` — the command and committed evidence satisfy the milestone
  contract.

## What Does Not Count as Completion

Reading, an uncommitted experiment, a screenshot without a reproducible
command, or an unsupported claim does not satisfy a completion gate.
