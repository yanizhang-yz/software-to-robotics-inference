# Portfolio Map

Convert completed, evidence-backed work into a case study. The map is a
planning tool: an item is not resume-ready until its evidence and limitations
are published.

| Artifact | Demonstrated skill | Case-study angle | Interview question | Resume bullet eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 committed skill map | Transferable systems reasoning | How software experience defined the learning sequence | Which existing skill changed your approach to a new domain? | Eligible when the map and selected route are committed |
| M1 reproducible measurement | Experiment design and inference measurement | Baseline, environment, and limits before optimization | How did you make a latency result trustworthy? | Eligible when the command and measured output are committed |
| M2 LeRobot inspection or simulation evaluation | Data and policy interpretation | Observations, actions, episodes, and policy behavior | How did data structure change your policy analysis? | Eligible when the inspected artifact and explanation are committed |
| M3 optional SO-ARM101 evaluation | Safety-aware physical-system delivery | Safe setup, failures, timing, and recovery | How did you bound risk during a robot evaluation? | Eligible only when the optional capstone has shareable evidence |
| M4 asynchronous loop test | Time-sensitive systems design | Backpressure, stale actions, and observability | What did your loop do under overload? | Eligible when test commands and recorded behavior are committed |
| M5 SGLang serving measurement | Model-serving tradeoff analysis | Prefill, decode, batching, memory, and latency | Which serving tradeoff changed your deployment choice? | Eligible when representative measurements and limitations are committed |
| M6 contribution and presentation | Open-source communication | Evidence turned into a reviewable engineering narrative | How did external review change your work? | Eligible when the contribution or presentation evidence is public |

For every eligible bullet, state only what the committed artifact supports.
Keep the command, environment, result, and limitation available for follow-up;
do not promote a planned, active, or blocked item as an outcome.
